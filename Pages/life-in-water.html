Our Oceans Need Protection

Oceans cover 71% of Earth, produce 50% of oxygen, and support 3 billion people. Yet they face pollution, overfishing, and climate impacts. Protecting aquatic life is critical for planetary health and human survival.

Support fishing practices that preserve marine ecosystems and fish populations.
