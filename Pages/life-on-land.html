Protecting Our Terrestrial Ecosystems

Forests cover 30% of Earth's land and are vital for biodiversity, carbon storage, and clean air. Deforestation, habitat loss, and unsustainable land use threaten countless species. By supporting conservation efforts and making sustainable choices, we can protect life on land.

💡 Quick Conservation Tips

🌳 Support Protected Areas
Visit and support national parks and protected reserves that preserve natural habitats and wildlife.
📦 Choose Sustainable Products
Look for FSC-certified wood, palm-oil-free products, and sustainable fashion made from natural materials.
🌿 Grow Your Own Food
Start a garden to reduce your food footprint, support local biodiversity, and enjoy fresh produce.
🔗 Donate to Conservation
Support organizations working to protect forests, wildlife, and endangered species worldwide.
